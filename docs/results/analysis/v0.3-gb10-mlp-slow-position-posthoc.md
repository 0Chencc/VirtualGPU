# GB10 long-read slow segments by sweep position

**Post-hoc. Decision (a), sweep context.** All three DRAM-related modes
have slow segments at the same exact point in at least three of five
host-qualified processes. The decision uses the preset tolerance of
two points, but the exact matches suffice. The frozen v0.2 conclusions
stand. This analysis does not qualify a profile field or change a
capture gate. [Host-gated capture](v0.3-gb10-mlp-host-gated-capture.md)

## Fixed rule and results

The input is the five frozen reports each for `mlp`, `strided-mlp`
and `dram-bw` from the host-gated session (freeze `be243f6a...`).
The analysis verifies the receipt digests, sweep order, geometry
and steps. It uses the original marker from the low-in-flight study:
a sample is `L` if its bandwidth is strictly below 85% of its
point's maximum. A segment is two or more consecutive `L` samples
within one point. Match segment-bearing points within two ordinals
of an anchor, counting each process once. Point and sample indices
below are one-based. The 90th-percentile reference gives the same
sets of matching anchors.

| Mode | Matching anchors within two points, at least 3/5 processes | Exact points, at least 3/5 | Exact points, 5/5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `mlp`, DRAM and L2 sections | 54/96 | 14 (11 DRAM, 3 L2) | 8 (all DRAM) |
| `strided-mlp` | 25/40 | 7 | 2 |
| `dram-bw` | 4/6 | 2 | 1 |

The counts are descriptive. Adjacent matching anchors can refer to
the same segment. The same geometry always occupies the same ordinal,
so the run-order match does not distinguish sequence history from
geometry sensitivity. The result meets the decision rule and calls
for separately reviewing any revised capture protocol before freezing
it. The low-in-flight exception in option (b) does not apply.

The representative recurring points contain both fast and slow
samples in each segment-bearing process. Their order includes
several slow-to-fast or fast-to-slow transitions, and the onset is
not usually at the same sample index. At the 128 KiB point the per-run
medians range from 193.59 to 218.21 GB/s as the slow fraction varies.
The contiguous DRAM 48 KiB case has slow-subset medians of 73.94 to
79.41 GB/s and fast-subset medians of 107.94 to 108.21 GB/s. The
64 KiB vector case has the corresponding ranges 92.19 to 106.12 and
136.53 to 137.27 GB/s. This is recurrence of within-run segments, with
some runs predominantly fast and others more mixed. A few exact
patterns recur, but there is no single start sample across the grid.

Every cold sample is preceded by its own read-only flush, including
samples at the first point. The table's prior point is the prior
geometry, not the operation immediately before the timed event.
Both small-to-small and large-to-small predecessors occur. The
contiguous and strided paths use a 256 MiB flush; `dram-bw` uses
96 MiB on this GB10. The accumulated durations exclude this flush,
the three warmups per point, device initialization and host gaps.
They give a timed-only offset from the first event, not an actual
elapsed offset from process start. The receipts have process UTC
bounds but no per-sample UTC times or SM placements.

## Where the IQR and two-cluster flags occur

The cells are IQR-flagged point-repeat observations over total
observations. Bounds after the first bucket are lower-exclusive.
The separate two-cluster flag uses the diagnostic split rule
(at least three observations on each side, a gap of at least 5% of
the median and more than 2.5 times the larger within-group IQR).
It describes sample shape and does not enter decision (a).

| Curve and issue path | Up to 64 KiB | 64 to 256 KiB | 256 to 512 KiB | 512 to 768 KiB | Above 768 KiB | Clear two-cluster among IQR flags |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DRAM contiguous, `cp_async` | 13/40 | 9/40 | 0/10 | 0/5 | 0/25 | 8/22 |
| DRAM contiguous, `ld_global_v4` | 8/40 | 10/40 | 0/10 | 0/5 | 0/25 | 10/18 |
| L2 contiguous, `cp_async` | 3/40 | 2/40 | 3/10 | 3/5 | 1/25 | 2/12 |
| L2 contiguous, `ld_global_v4` | 7/40 | 7/40 | 1/10 | 0/5 | 0/25 | 1/15 |
| DRAM strided, `cp_async` | 9/60 | 9/40 | 4/20 | 0/20 | 0/60 | 9/22 |

DRAM active-SM uses a separate axis: its flags are 5/5 at 1 SM, 3/5
at 2 SMs and 0/20 at 4 or more SMs. One of the eight passes the
two-cluster shape test; the broader spreads still show slow segments.
For DRAM contiguous, 35 of 40 flags use 4 KiB per CTA and five use
8 KiB; flagged grids range from 1 to 48 CTAs. For strided, the counts
are 18 at 4 KiB and four at 8 KiB, over 4 to 48 CTAs. At 384 KiB only
the strided curve flags (4/20); contiguous has none at either 384 KiB
or 768 KiB. The full per-point geometry and all 15 samples are in the
analysis JSON.

The fast/slow ratios for clear IQR-flagged clusters span 1.216 to 1.528
(median 1.417) in contiguous DRAM and 1.262 to 1.672 (median 1.389) in
strided DRAM. The L2 contiguous range is 1.184 to 1.215 (three lists).
There is no single fixed factor. Flagged launches span 1.169 to
3.211 ms in contiguous DRAM, 1.277 to 2.736 ms in strided DRAM, and
1.275 to 2.928 ms in active-SM DRAM. Those timed paths have 1 GiB,
2 GiB and 1 GiB source allocations respectively; their flagged
transfer volumes span 16 to 294 MiB, 39.06 to 288.75 MiB, and 81 to
151 MiB respectively. The warm L2 MLP path uses an 8 MiB working set.

## Telemetry and alternative contexts

All 15 processes pass the replayed host check. During capture, the
available NVML snapshots for these modes report 208 MHz SM clock
while P-states include P0 and P8; the warmup snapshots separately
report higher SM clocks. Memory clock reads `[N/A]` throughout.
Take the process snapshots as coarse observations: temperature spans
47 to 55 C, power 3.93 to 33.88 W, thermal slowdown is inactive, and
software power cap is marked active in some P0 and P8 samples.
These clock and event fields cannot be matched to individual CUDA
events, so they cannot establish a cluster-to-clock correspondence
or exclude a transient fabric state.

The [L2 bistability observation](v0.3-gb10-l2-bistability-observation.md)
concerns repeated traversals near 0.75 to 1.25 times the 24 MiB L2
capacity (18 to 30 MiB) across processes. The cold MLP source and
flush are distinct allocations. Consecutive source addresses do not
repeat within the measured cold launch, and the active-SM and
strided flagged transfer volumes are larger than the band. The 4 KiB
contiguous point flags with a 16 MiB transfer, while the 48 and
64 KiB examples transfer over 130 MiB. Bytes in flight is an
outstanding-request quantity, not the cache working set. The
observations supply no residency or hit-rate trace tying a split to
that known capacity band.

[Probe design and pilot](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe-plan.md) |

Two of the four formal probe sessions were available for this
analysis, each with 20 blocks. Across block medians, the same-kernel
plateau points' CV ranges were 0.23% to 1.21% and 0.25% to 1.20%.
Each session had one IQR flag in 280 point-block lists. The saturation
pilot places the onset of its plateau near 768 KiB, consistent with
the absence of cold curve flags in this range. The probe comparison
does not replay the low-in-flight geometries or their predecessors.
The two later formal sessions are outside this analysis snapshot.

The separate five-point instrumented trial reported in `bf04f1a`
does not reproduce the split in either original-condition process,
so its flush, rotation, stream and length contrasts are inconclusive.
The placement control also supplies no per-sample SM IDs for the
archived MLP runs. Together these comparisons make sweep context
worth isolating, but do not establish a particular clock, cache or
controller mechanism.
