# GB10 spill-latency: the redo of the allocation design and the serial 1 KiB exception (result)

Status: result of the check registered in [the redo registration](v0.3-spill-allocation-redo-registration.md), which reinstates the [allocation design](v0.3-spill-allocation-design-registration.md) and the [serial 1 KiB exception](v0.3-gb10-spill-serial-boundary-exception-registration.md) with one change: every reload point is sized to 2.0 ms in the pilot. **Verdict: adopted.** All 5 rounds were valid and passed rules 1 and 2, and rule 3 passed across them. As registered, this outcome is final.

The capture ended `passed` after 5 rounds, each one `valid`. Rule 1 judges 68 lists without the four excepted points, rule 2 judges all 72 lists, and rule 3 judges 68 points.

**The 1.0 ms floor.** The shortest real launch of the capture was 1.25 ms, on points sized to the unchanged 1.25 ms target. Among the reload points, the shortest was 1.41 ms (serial 4 KiB, 32 reloads). The serial 1 KiB points' shortest launches were 1.57 to 1.93 ms.

**Pilot of the serial 1 KiB point at 128 reloads.** The pilot tried 128, 278, 351 and 376 steps. At 376 steps its fastest launch was 2.52 ms, and it was frozen there. Its first trial included one fast launch (0.94 ms beside 2.10 and 2.20 ms), so the pilot did not see the slow state alone.

The merged output carries `"exceptions": ["l1_boundary_two_state"]` on exactly the four serial 1 KiB points with reloads.

## What this adopts

- **The adopted method.** `spill-latency` reads its reload points by the allocation design with the 2.0 ms pilot target, and GB10's serial 1 KiB reload points carry the exception.
- **The known limit.** A real kernel runs in one allocation. The 4 KiB and 2 KiB reload points span 31% to 42% and 15% to 26% between allocations (pooled). The profile's averaged value can therefore sit far from what one kernel meets, and the widened `rel_uncertainty` is meant to cover that.
- **design.md 9.2.** Its single-allocation wording, written after the withdrawal, no longer describes the adopted method. Changing it is left to the core owner.
- **Merging.** The implementation branch and the protocol document (`docs/components/long-launch-capture.md`) are merged and aligned separately, after this result.
