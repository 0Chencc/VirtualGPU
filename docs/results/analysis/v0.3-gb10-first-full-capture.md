# GB10 first full 0.23.0 capture

Measurement commit `42e039d93f8bf91d5ab2fda027240e5f144a9e68`.

`bb6813dae29d05e4bf3745017ba9ad7c01eabacb5a343a6ebfa2c058f2b7f4d2`.

**Capture failed.** All 26 modes finished the pilot and were frozen
together. The five initial sweeps and two permitted supplements recorded
136 mode-rounds, of which 17 were void. Three modes had fewer than five
valid rounds. Among the other 23 modes, the offline rule 3 check failed

is no 0.23.0 candidate or field-delta comparison with 0.22.0-posthoc.

## Capture and receipts

Spark 1 (GB10, `sm_121a`) used a detached checkout from a Git bundle,
CUDA 13.0.88 and driver 580.173.02. The native build ran under the
shared lock because Bifrost lacked a usable AArch64 CUDA C++ cross
toolchain. The pilot and timed capture also held the shared lock.
All 26 pilot processes passed the host gate. The shortest effective
pilot window was 1.250112 ms. The manifest and all schedules were
frozen at 2026-11-27 03:56:06 UTC. Formal process spans ran from
03:58:16.650 to 04:23:17.332 UTC, with 15 samples and `--clock-probe`.
All seven round-level thermal and host gates passed. The per-process
host check passed 135/136 times. The exception was round 6
`closed-loop-mma` at 10.22% peak aggregate CPU busy, also independently
void for frozen-geometry mismatch.

## Void rounds and supplements

The state machine ran all 26 modes in rounds 1 through 5. Rounds 6
and 7 both ran only `l2-merge`, `closed-loop-mma`, and `spill-latency`.
The campaign stopped at the two-supplement limit.

- `closed-loop-mma` had seven geometry mismatches. The pilot's
  `ns_per_mma_iter` was 27.278, and the captures' values ranged from
  27.506 to 27.619. The benchmark recalculates MMA iterations from this
  per-process calibration. At the first differing 4-warp point, the
  frozen target used 37 MMA iterations per stage and all seven captures
  used 36. Each capture disagreed at 12 to 42 of 150 points. The frozen
  identity check caught real changes in work. No timing from those
  rounds counted.
- `l2-merge` voided rounds 2, 3, 4 and 6 on rule 1, with 6 or 7 flags
  against 4 allowed. Rounds 1, 5 and 7 were valid.
- `spill-latency` voided rounds 1 to 5 and 7 on rule 1. Its sole valid
  round was 6, with 3 flags out of 3 allowed. Across all seven attempts,
  rule 2 stayed within its limit for both dispersion-failed modes.

## Reproducibility at the reported boundaries

- **L2 capacity sweep.** The 36 MiB point is 1.5 times the physical L2
  size and lies outside the registered exception. Round-median
  bandwidths were 1475.46, 882.03, 1166.95, 891.30 and 1150.07 GB/s.
  The rule 3 launch-time spread was 52.44%. The 48 MiB point also
  exceeded the per-point threshold at 21.40%. Together these flagged
  2 of 24 eligible points, exceeding the mode's allowance of 1.
- **L2 rescan at 1.5 times capacity.** The separate 36 MiB rescan
  point had pass-2 medians of 265.21, 240.94, 240.94, 241.09 and
  267.01 GB/s (10.81% spread relative to the median). Its formal
  rule 3 input is the grouped launch window. The mode passed that
  check in the offline replay with 0 of 6 eligible points flagged.
  The 36 MiB point received no exception in either mode.
- **Spill L1 boundary.** The 1 KiB frame serial-chain medians for 8,
  32, 68 and 128 reloads differed across attempts by 37.2%, 38.1%,
  21.3% and 27.3%, respectively, relative to the median of round
  medians. For the batched case the corresponding spreads were
  1.1%, 4.9%, 8.1% and 6.4%. These are descriptive results over all
  seven attempts, including the void rounds.

## Profile disposition

The 0.23.0 protocol requires all timed modes to come from one passed
capture with one frozen binary and manifest. A resumed interrupted
round remains part of that capture. Combining modes from independent
sessions or from different kernel revisions falls outside the
protocol. After PRs #19 and #20 change the spill and SFU/shuffle
kernels, a new candidate needs a full new qualification campaign.
This attempt records the first suite-wide results at `42e039d`.
The existing 0.22.0-posthoc profile stays installed.
