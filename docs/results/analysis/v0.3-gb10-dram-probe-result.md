# Result: the GB10 DRAM read probe, four formal sessions

**Registered analysis.** The [plan](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe-plan.md) and its pilot bounds were frozen before these sessions, and its decision rule is applied here unchanged, by [`microbench/probe_analyze.py`](../../../microbench/probe_analyze.py) (which marks its own output `post_hoc`, as every analysis tool here does). Two things were not in the plan and are reported as such: the host CPU check (`microbench/host_trace.py`), which ran from session 2 on, and one session postponed for another capture. No GEMM or attention timing enters anything here.

## Data

- Spark 1 (GB10), binary `b75ca0b` (`sha256` in each session file), driver as recorded there; `microbench/probe_blocks.py --blocks 20 --gap-seconds 30 --samples 15`, workload `mlp-plateau` (14 MLP plateau points, `cp_async` and `ld_global_v4`, 512 KiB to 7.5 MiB in flight), probes P1, P2, P3a and P3b before and after the workload in each block, all under one short lock per block.
- Sessions: [session-1.json](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/session-1.json) from 21:52 UTC on 2026-11-26, then [2](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/session-2.json) from 00:04, [3](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/session-3.json) from 02:24 and [4](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/session-4.json) from 04:27 on 2026-11-27. Session 4 was due at 04:46 and was postponed until the `0.23.0` GB10 capture released the GPU; it started at 04:25, 2 h 8 min after session 3 started and 1 h 39 min after it ended. The thermal gate passed before every session (GPU at 52 to 54 C, SM clock median 2496 to 2515 MHz).
- Host check ([host-judge.json](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/host-judge.json)): every block's window, from its state before to its state after, judged by the `host_trace.py` rule (total CPU busy share at most 10% in every sample, no other process above 5% of one CPU for two consecutive samples). Sessions 2 and 4 pass in all 20 blocks. In session 3, block 10 fails: a compiler (`cc1plus`) ran beside it for two samples; the block is voided. Session 1 ran before the sampler started; its blocks' own `/proc/stat` deltas show a busy share of 4.8% to 5.4% (the capture itself), and 9.9% in its last block, 19.
- No block was flagged as drift: in every block and for every candidate, the probes before and after differ by less than the frozen 3%.

The same counts follow with session 3's voided block kept ([analysis-with-voided-block.json](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/analysis-with-voided-block.json)) and with session 1's block 19 also left out ([analysis-without-s1-block-19.json](v0.3-gb10-dram-probe/formal/analysis-without-s1-block-19.json)), and the drift exclusion changes nothing since no block was flagged.

**Decision, by the frozen rule: the ratio is adopted** for this workload with P2, P3a and P3b at every point, and with P1 at 13 of 14.

What it does and does not show:

- The probe removes a drift that the session shares: absolute values move by about 4% between sessions hours apart, the ratio by well under 1%.
- Within a session the ratio is slightly noisier than the absolute value (0.47% to 1.03% against 0.38% to 0.91%), since it adds the probe's own noise; the gain is across sessions only.
- The cause of the drift is not known. Temperature and SM clock at the thermal gate were the same in all sessions; nothing recorded tracks it. Four sessions on one machine in one night, with the same monotone fall, are not a sample of all the states a profile capture can meet.
- The ratio is checked for MLP plateau reads beside the probe, not for other modes.

## What the profile gets

Agreed with the profile owner (5). The schema and the collector are a separate change by the owner, after review of this contract; this document changes neither.

- **The absolute fields stay.** No ratio is stored; a reader computes it from the absolute value and its reference.
- **Reference probe: P3a** (`ld.global.v4`, 4 loads in flight per thread). P2, P3a and P3b all pass at 14 of 14 points; P3a is chosen because it is separate from the `cp.async` implementation and is not itself one of the MLP points it would normalize. P2 and P3b stay in reports as controls. It is a session reference, not a ceiling: a ratio above 1 is allowed.
- **Reference value**, as reduced in this analysis: `value_gbps = (median(before samples) + median(after samples)) / 2`, the probe's median in the same block before and after the values it serves.
- **Optional top-level `references`, keyed by ID.** Each entry holds the device; `session_id` and `block_id`; the probe (geometry name, protocol and issue mode, active SMs, cache preparation); `value_gbps`; the before and after samples, each with its UTC; the source revision and binary digest; whether the block is valid or flagged (voided by a gate, or drift by the frozen 3% bound); and a digest of the raw capture.
- **Pairing.** Only a rate and a reference captured in the same locked block may be paired. A single block observation may carry a `reference_id`. An aggregated `MeasuredValue` over rounds does not carry one ID, and its value is never divided by one block's reference or by an aggregated reference.
- **Recomputing a normalized value.** The profile keeps each block observation as a pair (`reference_id`, `value_gbps`); the ratio is formed block by block first, then reduced over independent sessions, as here (median over blocks within a session, then over sessions). Errors of values sharing one reference share that denominator's error.
- **Voided and drift blocks** stay in the audit record under the frozen rules; qualified aggregates are formed as before. Existing `0.23.0` data gets no references after the fact.
- **Scope.** The evidence covers these 14 MLP plateau points on this GB10 only. Low bytes in flight, `closed-loop` and active-SM curves need their own same-block reproduction before a reference is used for them.

## Limits

- One GB10, four sessions in one night. Sessions start at least two hours apart; session 4 started 1 h 39 min after session 3 ended.
- Session 1 has no per-process host record.
- The intervals resample blocks within each session and keep the four sessions fixed, so they show block noise, not how much another set of sessions would differ. With four sessions a CV is coarse.
