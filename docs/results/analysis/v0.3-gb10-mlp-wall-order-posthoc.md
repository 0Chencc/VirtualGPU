# GB10 MLP wall-clock and order reversal result

**Post-hoc, unresolved.** The two forward replays did not reproduce
the same geometry or wall-clock onset. The nearest pair of forward
onsets is 397.672 ms apart. The reversed sweep therefore provides
no match for either prespecified branch. The frozen v0.2 conclusions
stand. [Frozen comparison plan](v0.3-gb10-mlp-wall-order-plan.md)

## Capture evidence

Spark 1 ran exactly three independent processes in the order forward,
forward, reverse, from 2026-11-27 01:36:12 to 01:36:25 UTC. The
capture used `67f7a4c` and binary SHA-256
`cc483dbc8bfc08fa95bf016a16c9b9f86a461e492358af95839e4178149aab41`.
The order manifest SHA-256 was
`402828098781ef2330fd775abedee6784290fcbe8e089cc04b45f37b92774955`.
Each process executed 96 points, three warmups and 15 timed samples
per point. Raw occurrence order, geometry keys, transfer lengths
and frozen steps agree. All three host gates passed, with peak
aggregate busy shares of 5.66%, 5.77% and 5.54%, respectively.

Direct comparison with the original frozen binary under
`cuobjdump -sass -arch sm_121` found identical instructions in all
ten MLP and flush symbols. Both replay orders call the same compute
kernels as the original path. Sample start and launch completion
are host monotonic observations. The flush-completion timestamp is
estimated from the launch endpoint and same-stream events with the
existing wait. Slow onsets use the actual host start timestamp.

## Recurrence and stopping decision

Using the same slow marker, the earlier five forward sweeps had 17,
14, 12, 16 and 16 cold DRAM segments. Their warm L2 sections had
6, 6, 7, 4 and 6. The three timestamped sweeps had 4, 1 and 1 cold
segments and zero in warm L2. Slow segments recur in the earlier
campaign and are sparse in the replay, even with the same timed
kernel instructions and frozen work per point. A single process
cannot characterize how often the effect appears. The campaigns
differ in timing instrumentation and session context, so the counts
do not establish the cause of the change.
[Earlier position analysis](v0.3-gb10-mlp-slow-position-posthoc.md)

The two timestamped forward sweeps also serve as the positive
control for the four-step separation design. Their onsets share no
point key and their nearest pair is 397.672 ms apart. The positive
control did not reproduce the recurring pattern. The separation
design stopped at step 1, with the already completed reversed sweep
retained as diagnostic evidence. The replay timestamps the timed
samples and cold flush events. It does not separately timestamp each
warmup. [Four-step separation design](v0.3-gb10-mlp-wall-time-posthoc.md)

## Registered GB10 exception

The approved unresolved branch registers low-in-flight instability
for GB10 only. It applies to the following fields and point keys
in a future qualified full-profile capture.

| Field | Points | Evidence from the five archived host-gated sweeps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dram_bw_by_bytes_in_flight` | Cold reads at or below 256 KiB total in flight, both issue paths | At or below the boundary, 40/160 lists exceeded the IQR threshold and there were 75 slow segments. Above it, 0/80 lists exceeded the threshold and there were zero segments. |
| `dram_bw_by_bytes_in_flight_strided` | Cold reads at or below 384 KiB total in flight | At 384 KiB, 4/20 lists exceeded the IQR threshold and ten slow segments occurred across all five sweeps, all at 128 B segments and 4 KiB stride. At the next sampled size, 768 KiB, 0/20 lists exceeded the threshold and there were zero segments. |
| `dram_bw_by_active_sms` | Target 1 or 2 SMs | At 1 and 2 SMs, respectively, 5/5 and 3/5 lists exceeded the IQR threshold, with 11 and five slow segments. At 4 SMs and above, 0/20 lists exceeded the threshold and there were zero segments. |

The strided sweep has no 512 KiB point. The archived 4/20 count for
the 256 to 512 KiB bin comes entirely from 384 KiB. Any future
512 KiB strided point is outside the exception. These boundaries are
fixed before the full-profile recapture and may not be revised
using its results.

Keep every sample and all process medians. At these points record
the min and max of the process medians, set `rel_uncertainty` to
cover the observed spread including both clusters, and tag the
point `low_in_flight_unstable`. Exclude these points from the
cohort decision for rules 1 and 3. Rule 2 still applies to every
point, and all three rules remain in effect outside the exception.
The earlier host-gated archive already failed rule 2 for DRAM
active-SM. This registration does not qualify that batch or replace
any installed profile measurement. The affected range includes
low-CTA, latency-limited decode geometries.
