# GB10 carveout probe, second registration

Registered by decision of the lead before any implementation change or run, as the only repeat of the probe registered in [v0.3-gb10-carveout-probe-registration.md](v0.3-gb10-carveout-probe-registration.md). Nothing below changes after the data are taken. That registration stands in every point not named here: the question, the four launch shapes and working sets of step 1, the frames, warps and five contexts of step 2, the acquisition with its gates and replacements, the handling of contexts without a crossing, and the readings with their thresholds and four outcomes, which are not changed.

## What the first run tells this registration

Only the published cause of the void first run is used here: every process failed `long_launch.verify` at step 1 `R16` with a 104 KiB working set, with an in-kernel window of 988 to 993 us under the 1.0 ms floor. The first run's data are archived and were not read. No threshold, reading or length in this registration refers to them.

## Change: every timed window sized to twice the floor

- **Step 1.** Every point, with no exception near any cliff, is sized by the pilot to an in-kernel window of at least twice the pilot floor, 2.5 ms, through the schedule's existing `observed_target_ns`, as `l2-rescan` does in GB10's bistable band.
- **Step 2.** By the same principle, every point is sized to a timed window of at least 2.5 ms. Its timed window is the launch, so the point reports the launch's event time to the same overload.
- **Capture floor.** It stays 1.0 ms for every window, and `long_launch.verify` judges it as before.

## Limits added

- **What the first run did not show.** `long_launch.verify` stops at the first point that fails. In the void run, whether any other point also fell short is therefore not known.
- **This run can still fail.** Twice the floor protects against a window that falls by up to half from the pilot's samples, not more. If any process fails `verify` again, the rules of the first registration apply (void, at most two replacements). If the run is void, there is no further repeat.

## Result

Measurement commit `dee7568` (local branch `s4/v03-carveout-probe-impl2` before its rebase; the rebased `536e454` is the same patch), binary SHA-256 `ed54922cd410f6b3a54c5cdaef1f9f5866399e3252c85e3f26588afc8cf6116a`, built on Spark 1 with CUDA 13.0 for `sm_121a`. One session on 2026-11-27 from 06:56:07 to 07:02:42 UTC, under the GPU lock for the transfer, the build and every run, after 7 and 8 had released it. The pilot froze 171 points at 06:57:49. The raw records are in `results/v03-gb10-carveout-probe/dee7568/spark-raw.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `53eb70e2...`) in the 4 worktree.

**Acquisition.** The first three processes are all valid, and no replacement was used.

- Every process passed the host gate and `long_launch.verify`. The shortest launch was 2.009 to 2.014 ms.
- Rule 1: 17, 21 and 19 of 438 lists, with 21 allowed. The second process is at the limit.
- Rule 2: 8, 6 and 6, with 65 allowed.

By the registered rule this reads "labelled by carveout": a 128-thread CTA with no shared memory request gets an L1 like the 16 KiB curve's, which is the curve the core reads for it. The labelling by request, inferred in the correction of `v0.3-gb10-first-capture-l2-diagnosis.md`, section 8, is not supported.

**Step 2 reading: no transition in the swept range.** In all 15 contexts m(w) stays below 0.5, between 0.015 and 0.044 at 6 to 10 warps, falling as the warps rise. No context has a crossing, so step 2 has no T, and step 1's reading stands alone.

**Reported, not judged.** The 512 B frames of the `spill-latency` kernel run at 1.5% to 4.4% of the way from the 128 B frame's per-step time to the 4096 B frame's, at footprints of 96 to 160 KiB per SM. That is, near the L1 level, although step 1 puts this shape's pointer-chase cliff at about 102 KiB. Why the spill frames do not leave L1 at these footprints is not known from this probe. That bears on the l1_to_l2 step the core applies to spill frames, and is left to the spill mechanism's owner and the spill decision of `ad1d505`.
