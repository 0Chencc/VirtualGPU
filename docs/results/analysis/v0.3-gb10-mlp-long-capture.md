The descriptive two-cluster flag sorts the 15 bandwidth samples and tries the largest gap that leaves at least three samples on each side. It requires a gap of at least 5% of the point median and greater than 2.5 times the larger within-group IQR. Other flagged points have no clear two-cluster split under this heuristic. The flag does not enter any acceptance rule.

## Host timeline

The original seven reports carry pre/post load averages, memory snapshots and process lists with lifetime CPU shares. They lack per-second CPU deltas. The resumed processes carry timestamped load average, aggregate `/proc/stat` counters and `/proc/<pid>/stat` process deltas. Their host checks cover intervals overlapping the capture process's timing window. Individual geometry points have CUDA event durations without UTC positions, so we cannot align a single point's slower samples with a particular CPU interval.

All 13 traced captures met the host CPU thresholds across warmup and timing. The 38 one-second or boundary intervals overlapping the timing windows had a one-minute load average of 0.91 to 1.41. Per-capture peak aggregate CPU busy share ranged from 5.15% to 7.16%. No outside process exceeded 5% of a core in two consecutive intervals. The per-process deltas remain in the archive. The last three contiguous, strided and DRAM active-SM runs still failed the IQR rule under those observed host conditions. CPU contention remains a known calibration risk, and the present traces neither explain the remaining spread nor cover brief effects below their sampling resolution.
